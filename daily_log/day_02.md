- **Notes**: Started documenting string concepts in `f_string_notes.md`.

## 💡 Key Learnings
- **F-Strings**: Introduced in Python 3.6, they provide a concise and readable way to embed expressions inside string literals.
- **In-place Methods**: You can call methods like `.title()`, `.lower()`, or `.upper()` directly inside the `{}` of an f-string.
- **Readability**: F-strings are generally more readable and faster than `%` formatting or `.format()`.
